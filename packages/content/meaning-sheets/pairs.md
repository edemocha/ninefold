# Between us: notes on writing about two numbers

Status: **draft for the owner's approval** (Gate P1). Nothing for Between us is drafted from this until the owner has read it and the voice-guide addendum. The text for each number lives in its own sheet (`1.md` to `33.md`); this page covers only what changes when two numbers sit side by side.

## What the feature is, and is not

Between us sets two people's numbers next to each other and shows the arithmetic that links them. It is for any two people: friends, family, colleagues, partners. It does not say whether two people are right for each other, because the research does not support that, and a verdict can do harm (see `docs/relationship-feature-plan.md`, section 2).

- It **describes numbers**, never people. "A 3 tends to say it out loud" is allowed. "Your partner is a talker" is not.
- It has **no score, rating, ranking or match**. A pair reads the same in either order.
- It **never says whether to begin, stay or part**. The lint blocks the words that usually introduce that.
- It ends each set of text on **questions each person answers about themselves**.

Master numbers (11, 22, 33) add one short note and use the root pair for the main text (11 with 2, 22 with 4, 33 with 6). They are themes, not ranks. Never say a master makes a pair special, rarer or better.

## Same number and different numbers

- **Two of the same number** meet their own strengths and their own shadow. Write what is easy (recognition) and what is amplified (the shared shadow), and keep the tone warm, not warning.
- **Different numbers** meet a difference in pace, in how they decide, or in how they like to be cared for. Name the difference neutrally and say what each side could try.

## The gap between two cycles

Two people's personal-year, month and day numbers sit a fixed number of steps apart on the nine-number cycle. The page shows the arithmetic: each person's birth month plus day, reduced to a digit, then the difference. Write only what is true of the arithmetic, then a symbolic reading:

- **0 steps:** the same number. They meet each phase together.
- **1 step:** neighbors. One is already in the theme the other is about to enter.
- **2 steps:** different themes in the same cycle.
- **3 steps:** a third of the way round. Different stages.
- **4 steps:** the widest gap. Two views of the cycle.

The gap says nothing about how two people get on. The `use` section turns it into something practical: share experience, plan around it, be curious.

## Talk prompts

- Ask each person about **themselves**: what they need, what they have trouble saying, what they would like to try.
- Prefer "What do you need before...?" and "When did you last...?" to anything about what the other person does.
- Never ask "why do you always...". Never imply a person is the problem.
- Two or three questions, ending on a question mark.

## Words to avoid in this feature

The lint enforces these only on Between us: compatible, incompatible, match, mismatch, soulmate, twin flame, meant to be, destiny, perfect, ideal, toxic, red flag, score, rating, rank, percent; partner, husband, wife, boyfriend, girlfriend, spouse, lover, romantic; he, she, him, her, his; narcissist, abusive, manipulative; attract, in love, fall for. The usual rules (certainty, fear, health, money, legal, relationship verdicts) still apply.
