# 6: Care and responsibility

**Keywords:** care, responsibility, home, service, harmony, beauty, loyalty, family and community

**Shadow side:** over-giving, perfectionism, meddling, resentment, control through helpfulness, taking on other people's load

**Verbs:** tend, host, mend, nurture, tidy, listen, show up

**Stance:** The 6 notices what needs looking after and does it. It makes places and groups feel held. The shadow is care that expects to be repaid, or that cannot stop at the edge of someone else's business.

**How to write it:** Ask who is looking after the carer. Treat boundaries as part of care.
