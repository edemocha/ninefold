# 2: Listening, pairing and patience

**Keywords:** cooperation, listening, partnership, sensitivity, patience, diplomacy, balance, tact

**Shadow side:** people-pleasing, indecision, over-sensitivity, leaning on others, avoiding conflict, keeping score quietly

**Verbs:** listen, pair, mediate, wait, soften, notice, support

**Stance:** The 2 reads the room. Strength comes from attention to other people and from patience; the cost is losing track of its own preference. Gentle, observant, and more stubborn underneath than it looks.

**How to write it:** Ask what they want when nobody is asking. Treat sensitivity as information, not weakness.
