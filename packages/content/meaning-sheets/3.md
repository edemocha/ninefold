# 3: Expression and play

**Keywords:** expression, creativity, humor, optimism, conversation, sociability, imagination, color

**Shadow side:** scattering, surface talk, mood swings, avoiding depth, performing instead of feeling, unfinished projects

**Verbs:** say, make, share, play, joke, draw, tell

**Stance:** The 3 puts things into words, images and company. It lifts a room and makes ideas easy to take in. Its practice is staying with something past the first burst, and saying the harder thing as well as the charming one.

**How to write it:** Ask what they are trying to say and who is listening. Keep humor in the writing, but do not make jokes about the reader.
