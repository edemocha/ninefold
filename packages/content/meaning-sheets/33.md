# 33: Devoted service and teaching

**Keywords:** devoted care, teaching, compassion in practice, mentoring, holding others, patience with people

**Shadow side:** self-sacrifice, taking on too much, perfectionism in care, martyrdom, ignoring one's own needs

**Verbs:** teach, mentor, serve, hold, encourage, listen, support

**Stance:** The 33 is a 6 stretched toward a wider circle: care that teaches. It is rare and easily over-claimed. The practice is service that has limits, and care that includes the carer.

**How to write it:** Present 33 as a symbolic theme, not a rank. Give the root 6 alongside it, and say plainly that 33 is the least settled number in the tradition.
