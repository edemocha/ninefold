# 4: Structure and steady work

**Keywords:** structure, order, discipline, practicality, reliability, foundations, patience with detail, craft

**Shadow side:** rigidity, stubbornness, overwork, resisting change, narrow focus, joyless routine, suspicion of the untried

**Verbs:** build, plan, repeat, check, organize, maintain, measure

**Stance:** The 4 builds the thing that is still standing next year. It trusts method and effort over inspiration. Its shadow is a plan held too tightly, and a day with no room in it.

**How to write it:** Ask what is worth building slowly and what has been kept only because it was always done that way. Praise the unglamorous work.
