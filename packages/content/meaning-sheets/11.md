# 11: Intuition and sensitivity

**Keywords:** intuition, sensitivity, inspiration, vision, idealism, a strong inner signal, receptivity

**Shadow side:** nervous strain, self-doubt, impracticality, over-sensitivity, anxiety, living in the idea instead of the day

**Verbs:** sense, notice, inspire, translate, steady, rest, ground

**Stance:** The 11 is a 2 turned up: the same listening, with a stronger signal. Insight comes fast; so does overload. The practice is grounding the signal in routine, rest and ordinary tasks.

**How to write it:** Treat 11 as a theme of heightened sensitivity and ideals, not as proof of gifts or a special fate. Give the root 2 alongside it.
