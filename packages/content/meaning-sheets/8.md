# 8: Effort and follow-through

**Keywords:** effort, structure, resources, follow-through, authority, ambition, results, stewardship

**Shadow side:** control, overwork, measuring worth by output, hardness, impatience with softness, keeping score

**Verbs:** finish, organize, negotiate, steward, deliver, commit, manage

**Stance:** The 8 turns intention into results through effort and structure. It is at home with responsibility and scale. Control and overwork are the shadow, along with the quiet belief that rest has to be earned.

**How to write it:** Ask what enough looks like. Do not promise success or wealth; talk about effort, structure and what it costs.
