# 5: Change, movement and freedom

**Keywords:** change, freedom, curiosity, adventure, adaptability, the senses, variety, speed

**Shadow side:** restlessness, impulsiveness, avoiding commitment, excess, scattering, boredom with follow-through

**Verbs:** move, try, travel, switch, explore, taste, sample

**Stance:** The 5 learns by going and trying. It keeps things from going stale and handles surprise well. Its practice is staying put long enough for a choice to pay off, and choosing freedom on purpose rather than as an escape.

**How to write it:** Ask what they are moving toward, not only away from. Do not romanticize drifting.
