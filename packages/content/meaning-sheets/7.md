# 7: Reflection and study

**Keywords:** reflection, study, solitude, analysis, depth, inner life, curiosity about how things work, quiet

**Shadow side:** withdrawal, aloofness, over-analysis, secrecy, cynicism, waiting for certainty that does not come

**Verbs:** study, wonder, retreat, question, test, read, watch

**Stance:** The 7 wants to understand before acting. It brings depth, careful thought and a good nose for what does not add up. Its practice is letting people in, and acting before every question is answered.

**How to write it:** Ask what they have been thinking about and who knows it. Respect the need for quiet without treating isolation as wisdom.
