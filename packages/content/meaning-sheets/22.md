# 22: Building at scale

**Keywords:** practical vision, large-scale building, discipline with ambition, systems, legacy, patience, organizing many people

**Shadow side:** pressure, overreach, self-doubt, workaholism, fear of falling short, holding the vision too tightly

**Verbs:** plan, build, organize, ground, delegate, sustain, scale

**Stance:** The 22 is a 4 with a larger horizon: the same method, applied to something that has to outlast one person's effort. The shadow is the weight of expecting too much of oneself.

**How to write it:** Talk about patience and pace. Do not tell anyone they are destined to build something great. Give the root 4 alongside it.
