# 1: Beginnings and self-direction

**Keywords:** initiative, independence, courage, originality, self-reliance, starting, standing alone

**Shadow side:** stubbornness, going it alone, impatience, bossiness, difficulty asking for help, defensiveness

**Verbs:** start, choose, lead, decide, try first, claim, stand

**Stance:** The 1 is the first move. It is the person who starts before the group is ready, and who has to learn that a start is not a finish and that other people are not obstacles. Warm, direct and a little self-contained.

**How to write it:** Ask about what they want to begin and who they are asking for permission. Do not praise toughness as if it were the whole point.
