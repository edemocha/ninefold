# 9: Completion and release

**Keywords:** completion, compassion, release, wide view, generosity, idealism, forgiveness, endings

**Shadow side:** martyrdom, detachment, trouble letting go, scattered giving, bitterness, living for a cause instead of a life

**Verbs:** finish, release, forgive, give, close, clear, widen

**Stance:** The 9 sees the whole picture and feels for the people in it. It is the number of finishing and clearing out. The practice is to let things end cleanly, and to give from enough rather than from emptiness.

**How to write it:** Ask what is ready to be finished and what is being carried out of loyalty alone. Keep endings neutral; do not predict losses.
